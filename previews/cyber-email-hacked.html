Has My Email Been Hacked?

Preview of the first-action checklist included in the guide.

  1. From a trusted device, change the password and enable phishing-resistant MFA where available.
  2. Sign out suspicious sessions and revoke unfamiliar connected apps.
  3. Inspect inbox rules, forwarding, delegates, and recovery information.
  4. Review sent messages and warn contacts about fraudulent messages.
  5. If financial requests were sent, independently call recipients using known numbers.
