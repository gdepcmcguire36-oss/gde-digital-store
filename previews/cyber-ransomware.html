Ransomware Emergency Survival Guide

Preview of the first-action checklist included in the guide.

  1. Disconnect affected devices from wired and wireless networks; do not wipe them.
  2. Notify your incident response lead, IT provider, and cyber insurer; use a trusted communication channel.
  3. Record ransom notes, file extensions, affected assets, timestamps, and suspected entry points.
  4. Protect unaffected backups and assess scope with qualified responders.
  5. Restore only from verified clean backups after containment and remediation.
