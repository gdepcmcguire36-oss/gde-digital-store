Lost or Stolen Laptop Response Plan

Preview of the first-action checklist included in the guide.

  1. Notify IT and security staff with last known location and time.
  2. Use approved device management to lock or wipe remotely where appropriate.
  3. Revoke sessions and credentials accessible from the device.
  4. Determine whether encryption was enabled and what data may be exposed.
  5. File a police report when appropriate and document the incident.
