Microsoft 365 Account Recovery Guide

Preview of the first-action checklist included in the guide.

  1. Verify suspicious sign-ins in Entra ID with authorized administrator access.
  2. Reset compromised credentials and revoke active sessions.
  3. Review MFA methods, app consents, mailbox forwarding and inbox rules.
  4. Audit administrative changes and suspicious outbound messages.
  5. Escalate to Microsoft support or a qualified administrator if tenant access is lost.
