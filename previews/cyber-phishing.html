Clicked a Phishing Link: Action Checklist

Preview of the first-action checklist included in the guide.

  1. Stop interacting with the page or attachment.
  2. Tell your IT or security contact what you clicked and when.
  3. If credentials were entered, change them from a trusted device and revoke sessions.
  4. If a file ran, isolate the device and obtain professional assistance.
  5. Monitor accounts and financial activity for suspicious changes.
