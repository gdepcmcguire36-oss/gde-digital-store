Digital downloads and support

Our guides are downloadable digital resources. Preview pages are free to view. Full paid files are supplied only after payment verification and when delivery is enabled.

How delivery will work

  1. Choose a product and complete secure Stripe checkout.
  2. After successful payment, follow the confirmation-page instructions to access the purchased PDF.
  3. Save a copy to your device for personal or internal-business use.

Having trouble?

Keep your Stripe receipt and contact gary@gdecomputers.com with the product name, payment date and order reference. Never email full card details or passwords.
